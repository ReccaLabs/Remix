About Recca Labs

Software built for Sri Lankan tuition, not adapted from abroad.

Recca Labs is a software company in Colombo. ReMix is our product: one system for the hall, the Zoom room and the fee counter.

photo · the Recca Labs team with pilot teachers

Why we built ReMix

Tuition in Sri Lanka runs on halls, WhatsApp groups, bank slips and Zoom links. Most tools were built for somewhere else, so teachers patch four of them together and still lose lessons to Telegram and fees to shared accounts.

We built one system around how classes here actually work: month-by-month fees, mixed hall and online batches, cashiers on a Saturday morning and students on low-cost phones.

What we hold ourselves to

01Trust firstYou hand us your income and your students' data. We keep it calm, precise and honest.
02Fast for busy peopleEvery common task in three clicks or fewer, and keyboard shortcuts for the fee counter.
03Works on a cheap phoneStudent screens load quickly on a low-end Android over 4G.
04Three languages from day oneSinhala and Tamil written by native speakers, not machine-translated.
