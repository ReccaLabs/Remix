# Status report — 5 Oct 2026 (development paused)

> Part of the [ReMix development plan](README.md). Written when development paused because the Claude and Codex weekly limits were nearly used up. Read this first when work resumes.

## 1. Where we are in one paragraph

ReMix has a working, tested foundation and the first two product areas. An institute can be created, staff sign in with 2-step, students sign in with the 2-device limit, owners import students from CSV, set up classes, halls and a timetable, and take money at the counter: monthly invoices are generated, cashiers take cash or record bank transfers/cheques, receipts are numbered, printed on an 80 mm printer or downloaded as PDF, and every payment lands in one append-only ledger. Students have short IDs (`NIL-26-0042`) and printable cards (barcode, QR, NFC) that the cash counter scans. Online payment (PayHere), bank-slip upload, SMS and everything after money (lessons, Zoom, websites, platform admin, hosting) is not built yet. Everything runs on local staging; nothing is deployed.
